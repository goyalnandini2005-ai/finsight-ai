🔗 Connect Live Google Finance Sheet Bridge

Stream real-time NSE/BSE/NASDAQ prices directly from official =GOOGLEFINANCE() formulas.

How this works (Zero API keys, 100% Free & Official):
  1. Open a blank Google Sheet and put Ticker names in Column A and =GOOGLEFINANCE() in Column B: 
    TCS        | =GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:TCS", "price")
RELIANCE   | =GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:RELIANCE", "price")
AAPL       | =GOOGLEFINANCE("NASDAQ:AAPL", "price")
MSFT       | =GOOGLEFINANCE("NASDAQ:MSFT", "price")
TSLA       | =GOOGLEFINANCE("NASDAQ:TSLA", "price")
  2. In Google Sheets, click File → Share → Publish to web, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), and copy the link.
  3. Paste your published CSV link below—FinSight AI will pull real-time Google Finance prices for all tickers!
